How to subscribe

  1. Apple Calendar / Outlook: click Subscribe. Your calendar app takes it from there.
  2. Google Calendar: copy the .ics link, then Other calendars → Subscribe from URL. Google refreshes external feeds on its own schedule, often once a day.
  3. Do not download and import the file. Importing makes a one-time copy that never updates.
